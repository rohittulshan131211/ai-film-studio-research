# Contributing to AI Film Studio Research

Thank you for your interest in testing or contributing to this prototype research project.

## Who can contribute

Independent creators, editors, prompt designers, researchers, and developers are welcome to share feedback on structured AI-video production workflows.

## Useful contributions

- Test a workflow using a short script or creative brief.
- Identify unclear steps, missing information, or confusing terminology.
- Share examples of asset-consistency or prompt-management problems.
- Suggest improvements to the review checklist or export structure.
- Report failures with enough context to reproduce the workflow issue.

## How to report a workflow issue

Please include:

1. A short description of the intended result.
2. The stage where the issue occurred: planning, assets, prompt, generation, review, or export.
3. The observed issue.
4. Any relevant references or prompt structure, with private or sensitive material removed.
5. What outcome would have been more useful.

## Principles for contributors

- Do not upload personal, confidential, copyrighted, or sensitive material without permission.
- Do not report results as research findings unless they are documented and reproducible.
- Keep feedback specific, respectful, and focused on the workflow.
- The creator retains final responsibility for all creative and publishing decisions.

## Prototype notice

This is an early research prototype. Features, schemas, and documentation may change as evidence and creator feedback are gathered.
